The HFGCS EAM DAILY COMPILATION RECORDINGS PROJECT (DCRP) is the first attempt by NEET INTEL to collect recordings of EAMs broadcast on the HFGCS and share them with a wider audience. Unlike the later NEET INTEL DAILY TIMECARD PROJECT (DTCP), this series does not necessarily reflect comprehensive 24/7 HFGCS monitoring, and many of the videos represent a “whatever I happened to hear that day” effort, though the series does become more ambitious by its end (setting up for the more ambitious DTCP). Originally 100+1 videos (owing to 23022 being split into two parts), an additional 7 videos (after the series concluded) that are functionally similar have been classified as part of this project.

YYMMDD — filter to a date. Partials work: 2501 lights up all of January 2025, a full date singles out that day. - matches any digit: --06 = every June, ----01 = every 1st of the month.

C### — filter to days that broadcast an EAM of that character count, e.g. C120 (suggestions list the counts that exist). Combine any number: C39,52,47 = days with any of them — days with all stay brightest. Double the comma for only days with all: C32,,27.

G# — filter by active groups: G2 = days with Group 2, G23 = days with both 2 and 3. Days with exactly that set stay brightest.

.YYMMDD — jump without filtering: .2501 scrolls to January 2025 in place.

& — combines filters. e.g. 25&C292 filters for days in 2025 with a 292 char EAM.

Append x to collapse unrelated weeks instead of fading them: 2501x, C32,27x.

Searches work as URL parameters too: ?250111, ?c32,,27x.

Just start typing — no click needed. Enter opens the first match, Esc clears.
