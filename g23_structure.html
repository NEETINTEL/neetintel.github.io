G2/G3 EAM Structure

G2・G3 EAMの構造

IndexG4 EAM Structure
目次G4 EAMの構造

Group 3's structured messages are all put together the same way: the 2-character prefix, an opening block that doesn't repeat, then blocks of 5, 14 or 23 characters in which some slots hold the same letter in every block, then a short tail. Group 2 uses the 5-character blocks too, three at a time, and a G3 32 aired within a few minutes of one keeps the same slots.

グループ3の構造のあるメッセージは、どれも同じ組み立て方です。2文字のプレフィックス、繰り返さない最初のブロック、5文字・14文字・23文字のどれかのブロックの連なり（いくつかのスロットはどのブロックでも同じ文字）、そして短い末尾です。グループ2にも5文字のブロックが3つずつ現れ、その数分以内に放送されたG3の32文字は同じスロットを保ちます。

5文字のブロック G3 52件

Every G3 message where three or more 5-character blocks follow one another, each sharing 3 or more letters, in the same slots, with the next. By chance you would expect about none in the whole corpus. Rows are in date order, lined up on the first block. Since the break, 28 of 44 start their blocks at character 17 and 7 at character 20 (the 35s and 40s); before it they start at character 22 or later. In the 35 that start at 17 or 20, the 4th slot is never constant.
